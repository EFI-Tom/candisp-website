Set up Blitzer.de PRO for CanDisp

Configure Blitzer.de PRO notifications so CanDisp can display the app's alerts.

Step-by-step setup

  1. Open Blitzer.de PRO.
  2. Open the app menu.
  3. Open settings (Einstellungen).
  4. Open general settings (Allgemein).
  5. Enable showing the app on the lock screen (App im Sperrbildschirm anzeigen).
  6. Scroll down to notifications (Benachrichtigungen).
  7. Enable alert notifications (Benachrichtigungen für Warnungen).
  8. Open the display behavior option (Anzeigeverhalten) directly below.
  9. Select permanent display (Dauerhaft).

Important

CanDisp receives alerts through Android notifications. Blitzer.de PRO only sends these notifications when the app is in the background.

Lock your phone or keep another app in the foreground.

Start the app in the background

In general settings (Allgemein), you can enable starting the app in the background (App im Hintergrund starten). Blitzer.de PRO then runs in the background and its alerts can appear on CanDisp.
